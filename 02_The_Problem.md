# Problem Definition

## Problem Statement

Workers with variable income often lack a simple, centralized way to understand, organize, and verify their earnings.

Income may vary daily, weekly, or seasonally depending on schedules, tips, commissions, contracts, or multiple jobs.

As a result, many workers rely on several disconnected tools to track their finances.

---

# Current Challenges

Many variable-income workers currently depend on:

- Payroll portals
- Bank statements
- Handwritten notes
- Screenshots
- Spreadsheets
- Multiple employer apps

Because information is scattered, users often spend unnecessary time piecing together their financial picture.

---

# User Pain Points

## Tracking Earnings

Users struggle to understand how much they have earned across different shifts, jobs, or pay periods.

---

## Organization

Income records are often stored in multiple places instead of one organized system.

---

## Income Visibility

Without clear summaries, it becomes difficult to answer questions such as:

- How much did I make this month?
- Which days earn me the most?
- Which job is the most profitable?

---

## Income Verification

Providing proof of income for apartments, loans, or other financial needs can require gathering documents from several different sources.

---

# Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current tools typically focus on one piece of the problem.

Examples include:

- Payroll systems
- Banking apps
- Budgeting apps
- Employer scheduling platforms

Few solutions are designed specifically around the needs of workers whose income changes frequently.

---

# Opportunity

There is an opportunity to create a single platform that helps users:

- Track earnings
- Organize income records
- Understand earning patterns
- Prepare income documentation
- Gain greater confidence in their financial information

---

# Success Criteria

A successful solution should help users:

- Spend less time tracking income
- Better understand their earnings
- Keep financial records organized
- Access income information quickly
- Feel more confident managing variable income
