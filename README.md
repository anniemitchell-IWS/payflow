# PayFlow

> Helping variable-income workers better understand, organize, and verify their earnings.

---

## Overview

PayFlow is an independent product management case study exploring how technology can simplify income tracking for people whose earnings vary from shift to shift or come from multiple income sources.

Originally inspired by restaurant servers, the concept evolved into a broader solution for contractors, nurses, freelancers, commission-based professionals, and other workers with variable income.

Rather than simply recording earnings, PayFlow focuses on helping users understand income patterns, organize documentation, and make income easier to verify.

---

## The Opportunity

Managing variable income is often fragmented.

Workers frequently rely on payroll portals, handwritten notes, screenshots, spreadsheets, and bank statements to piece together their earnings.

PayFlow explores how a single platform could simplify this experience.
